Before
you visit.

A few things to consider when planning your alterations.

What clothing can I ask about?

Our services include dress alterations, suit and pant tailoring, shirt adjustments, and bridal and prom dress fitting. For a particular fabric or adjustment, call to discuss your garment.

What should I bring for a fitting?

Bring the garment you want altered. The shoes and undergarments you plan to wear with it can help when checking length and fit, especially for dresses and pants.

How long will my alterations take?

Timing depends on the garment and the changes needed. Call us to discuss your alterations and any date you have in mind before making plans.

How can I ask about pricing or a visit?

Call (512) 451-0292 or email info@exclusivealterations.com. Describe the garment and what you would like adjusted, and ask about the next step.
