A few things to know about choosing your wash.

What’s the difference between the washes?

With self-service, you use the high-pressure wash jets yourself. The touchless automatic bay cleans your vehicle’s exterior without brushes making contact with it.

Which wash should I choose?

Choose self-service if you want control over where you spray. Choose the touchless automatic wash if you prefer a hands-free exterior clean. Always follow the instructions posted at the bay.

Where can I find you?

We’re on MLK in Austin, right across from Whataburger. Use the Google Maps link on this page for directions.

Will the automatic bay fit my vehicle?

Check the clearance limits and instructions posted at the bay before entering, especially if you have a roof rack or other accessories. If you’re unsure, email us before your visit.

How can I check hours, pricing, or payment options?

Email thecarwashatmlk@gmail.com for current details before heading over. Follow the pricing and payment instructions displayed at the wash.
