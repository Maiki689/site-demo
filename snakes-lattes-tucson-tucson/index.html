A board game bar in Main Gate Square

Snakes & Lattes Tucson is a board game bar and restaurant in the Main Gate Square area, at 988 East University Boulevard. The bar has thousands of playable board games.

Food and drinks are served in the restaurant, so you can eat and drink while you play.

The space welcomes walk-ins, group dining, and online ordering for pickup.

Services

  • Board game bar and restaurant

    Thousands of playable board games, plus food and drinks.

  • Food and drinks

  • Online ordering for pickup

    Booking is available for events and group dining.
