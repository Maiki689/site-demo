Craft beer and fine wine in Central Phoenix

Goodz & Brews is a shop in Central Phoenix that sells craft beer and fine wine. It is open every day from 10am to 11pm, so local customers can stop in at any time during those hours.

The shop focuses on these two drinks and nothing else. You can find it at 2927 North 24th Street.
