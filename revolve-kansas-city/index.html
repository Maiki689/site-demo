About Revolve

Revolve is a bike shop on Troost Avenue where you can buy used bicycles, bicycle accessories, and bicycle clothing.

We also accept bicycle donations and offer an Earn-a-Bike program for the community. Stop by to check out what’s available or drop off a donation.

  • Community focus

    Shop supports local bike donations.

  • Sustainable choice

    Used bikes give bikes new life.

  • Bike program

    Earn-a-Bike program available onsite.
